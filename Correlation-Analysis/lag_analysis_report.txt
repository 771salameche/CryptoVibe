# Lag Analysis Report


This report identifies the optimal time delay (lag) where social media sentiment shows the strongest correlation with cryptocurrency price changes.


## Summary of Findings

| Crypto   |   Optimal Lag (Days) |   Pearson r |   P-value | Significant?   | Interpretation                                |
|:---------|---------------------:|------------:|----------:|:---------------|:----------------------------------------------|
| BTC      |                    3 |     -0.3114 |    0.0447 | Yes            | Significant correlation found at a 3-day lag. |
| ETH      |                    1 |     -0.2597 |    0.0926 | No             | No statistically significant lag found.       |
| SOL      |                    3 |      0.3496 |    0.0232 | Yes            | Significant correlation found at a 3-day lag. |


## Recommendations & Insights

- **BTC:** A statistically significant negative correlation exists when sentiment leads price by **3 day(s)**. This suggests that shifts in sentiment may be a useful predictor for this asset.
- **ETH:** No significant predictive link was found between daily sentiment and future price changes within the tested lag period.
- **SOL:** A statistically significant positive correlation exists when sentiment leads price by **3 day(s)**. This suggests that shifts in sentiment may be a useful predictor for this asset.
